# Retail Sales Data — Exploratory Data Analysis

## Objective

Performed exploratory data analysis on retail sales data to identify sales trends, customer purchasing patterns, product performance, and business insights.

## Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Google Colab

## Dataset

Retail Sales Dataset

## Analysis Performed

* Inspected and cleaned the dataset
* Performed descriptive statistical analysis
* Analyzed monthly and quarterly sales trends
* Studied customer age and gender distribution
* Analyzed product-category performance
* Examined revenue and quantity sold
* Performed correlation analysis
* Created visualizations to identify meaningful patterns

## Key Finding

Clothing recorded the highest quantity sold, followed by Electronics and Beauty.

## Business Insights

The analysis can support:

* Better inventory planning
* Customer segmentation and targeting
* Promotional strategy development
* Product-level decision-making
* Identification of sales trends and patterns

## Conclusion

The exploratory analysis provided a better understanding of sales performance, customer behavior, and product-category trends. These insights can help businesses make data-driven decisions related to inventory, marketing, and product strategy.
